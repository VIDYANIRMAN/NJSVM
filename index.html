Class 9 Science Chapter Notes

Comprehensive notes, key formulas, definition summaries, and diagrams for NJSVM school exams.

1. Physics Curriculum Notes 4 Chapters
📍 Chapter 1: Motion (गति)

Distance vs Displacement: Distance is scalar (total path length), while displacement is vector (shortest distance between initial and final position).

Equations of Motion:
1. v = u + at
2. s = ut + ½ at²
3. v² = u² + 2as

Uniform Circular Motion: Motion of an object along a circular path with constant speed. Speed = 2πr / T.

🛡️ Chapter 2: Force & Laws of Motion (बल तथा गति के नियम)

Newton's 1st Law (Inertia): An object remains in rest or uniform motion unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.

Newton's 2nd Law: Force = mass × acceleration (F = ma). Rate of change of momentum is directly proportional to applied force.

Newton's 3rd Law: To every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

⚙️ Chapter 3: Work, Energy & Simple Machines (कार्य, ऊर्जा एवं सरल मशीनें)

Work Done: W = F × s × cos(θ). SI unit is Joule (J).

Kinetic Energy: KE = ½ mv². Potential Energy: PE = mgh.

Law of Conservation of Energy: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it only transforms from one form to another.

🔊 Chapter 4: Sound Waves (ध्वनि तरंगें)

Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave created by vibrations. It requires a material medium to propagate.

Wave Relation: Speed (v) = Frequency (ν) × Wavelength (λ).

Audible Range for Humans: 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz (20 kHz).

2. Chemistry Curriculum Notes 3 Chapters
🧪 Chapter 1: Exploring Mixtures & Separation (मिश्रण एवं पृथक्करण)

Heterogeneous vs Homogeneous: Homogeneous mixtures have uniform composition throughout (e.g. salt solution), whereas heterogeneous mixtures do not (e.g. sand in water).

Separation Techniques: Distillation (miscible liquids with boiling point difference), Centrifugation (density differences), Chromatography (solubility differences).

⚛️ Chapter 2: Journey Inside the Atom (परमाणु की आंतरिक संरचना)

Subatomic Particles: Protons (+1, nucleus), Neutrons (0, nucleus), Electrons (-1, orbiting in shells).

Bohr's Model: Electrons revolve in discrete energy levels designated as K, L, M, N shells with maximum capacity 2n².

🧱 Chapter 3: Foundation of Matter (द्रव्य की नींव)

Law of Conservation of Mass: Mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

Law of Constant Proportions: In a chemical compound, elements are always present in definite proportions by mass.

3. Biology Curriculum Notes 4 Chapters
🔬 Chapter 1: The Fundamental Unit of Life - Cell (कोशिका)

Cell was discovered by Robert Hooke in 1665 in cork slices.

Key Organelles: Mitochondria (Powerhouse of the cell - ATP), Lysosomes (Suicide bags), Nucleus (Brain of the cell).

🌿 Chapter 2: Tissues (ऊतक)

Plant Tissues: Meristematic (dividing) & Permanent (Simple: Parenchyma, Collenchyma, Sclerenchyma; Complex: Xylem, Phloem).

Animal Tissues: Epithelial, Connective (Blood, Bone), Muscular, Nervous.

🌱 Chapter 3 & 4: Reproduction & Diversity in Living Organisms

Cell Division: Mitosis (for growth & repair - produces 2 identical diploid cells), Meiosis (for gamete formation - produces 4 haploid cells).

5 Kingdom Classification (Whittaker): Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia.
