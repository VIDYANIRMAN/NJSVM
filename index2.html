Class 9 Science Comprehensive Notes

Detailed syllabus notes aligned with CBSE & NCERT curriculum for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

Physics

1. Motion

Distance is scalar; Displacement is vector. Speed = Distance / Time. Velocity = Displacement / Time. Equations of motion: v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as.

  • • Uniform Motion: Equal distance in equal time intervals.
  • • Non-Uniform Motion: Velocity changes with respect to time (Acceleration).
Physics

2. Force and Laws of Motion

Newton's First Law (Inertia), Second Law (F = ma), and Third Law (Action & Reaction are equal and opposite). Momentum p = mv.

  • • Law of Conservation of Momentum: Total momentum before collision = Total momentum after collision.
Physics

3. Work, Energy & Sound

Work W = F × d cos(θ). Kinetic Energy = ½mv², Potential Energy = mgh. Sound travels as longitudinal mechanical waves requiring a material medium.

  • • Frequency (f) = 1 / T. Wave Speed v = λ × f.
Chemistry

4. Matter & Mixtures

States of matter: Solid, Liquid, Gas, Plasma, Bose-Einstein Condensate. Solutions, Suspensions, and Colloids (Tyndall Effect).

  • • Evaporation causes cooling due to latent heat absorption.
Chemistry

5. Atoms and Molecules

Law of Conservation of Mass & Law of Definite Proportions. Atomic mass unit (u). Concept of Mole: 1 Mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles.

  • • Chemical formula creation using valency matching.
Biology

6. Cell & Tissues

Cell is the structural & functional unit. Organelles: Mitochondria (Powerhouse), Lysosome (Suicide bag), Ribosome (Protein synthesis).

  • • Plant vs Animal Cells: Cell wall & Chloroplast presence.
  • • Tissues: Meristematic & Permanent plant tissues; Epithelial, Connective, Muscular, Nervous animal tissues.
